# Scripture of the Day

One hundred verses cleared for reading on air, in the **Jubilee Standard Version (JSV)**.

Source: `W:\ZevInspire.com\translation` — one folder per book, one file per
chapter. That tree is the authority for the wording here. If it changes, this file is
regenerated from it rather than edited:

```

```

## Before anyone reads one of these out

**The JSV is a translation in progress, and not all of it is fit to broadcast.** That is
why this is a fixed list rather than an instruction to open the Bible and pick something.

Parts of the text still carry the translator’s working alternatives inside the verse,
semicolon-separated and often duplicated. They are margin notes, not readings:

```
  1 John 4:19    We love; covenant love; covenant love; covenant love because He
                 first loved us.
  Hebrews 13:8   Yeshua the drenched with oil; saturated with Ruach One is the same
                 yesterday and today and forever.
  Proverbs 3:5   Trust in Yahuah with all your heart; your discerning sight; the
                 ability to distinguish; your discerning sight ...
  Psalms 33:4    For the davar of Yahuah; the word of Yahuah is upright ...
```

Every verse below was checked against seven faults and carries none of them:

| Fault | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| Duplicated gloss | `love; covenant love; covenant love` |
| Gloss tail | `a strong tower; the wholesome one` |
| Gloss echo | `the davar of Yahuah; the word of Yahuah` |
| Doubled word | `receive tender tender compassion` |
| Truncated verse | text that stops on a comma |
| No terminal punctuation | Ephesians 2:8 stops mid-sentence |
| Opens mid-sentence | Ephesians 2:9 is the bare tail `not from works...` |

Hebrew parallelism is **not** treated as a fault. “I will be exalted among the nations,
I will be exalted in the land” repeats on purpose, and so does “they are new every
morning; great is Your faithfulness.” Those are the verse, not an error in it.

Some very familiar references are missing, and it is worth knowing why rather than
assuming they were overlooked. **Romans 5:8, 1 Corinthians 13:4, Galatians 5:22,
Ephesians 2:8, Ephesians 2:10, Philippians 4:6, Jeremiah 29:11 and 1 John 1:9** are all
unreadable in the JSV as it stands. **Joshua 1:9** is absent because Joshua has no JSV
text in the tree yet, and neither does Job. They belong here the day that changes.

### The names, so nobody is caught out mid-sentence

The JSV uses Hebraic naming throughout:

| In the JSV | What other versions render |
|---|---|
| **Yahuah** | the LORD |
| **Elohim** | God |
| **Yeshua** | Jesus |
| **Ruach** | Spirit, breath |
| **shalom** | peace, wholeness |
| **Yisra’el**, **Ya’aqov**, **Yochanan** | Israel, Jacob, John |

Read them as written. Substituting the familiar word halfway through is what makes a
reading sound uncertain, and a fixed list exists so nothing has to be improvised.

### Using them on air

- One a day, in order, is a little over three months without a repeat.
- The themes are the useful handle, not the numbering: reach for **When You Are Afraid**
  on a hard news day, **Praise and Thanks** on a Sunday morning.
- Each is short enough to read between tracks without a talk-up, and self-contained
  enough to make sense to somebody who has just tuned in.
- Give the reference first and the verse second. Anyone who wants to find it later
  needs the address before the words.

## If you extend it

Add references to `THEMES` in the build script and re-run it. Do not add a verse to
this file by hand: the check is what the file is for, and a hand-added verse has not
been through it.

If a theme comes up short, the script says so by name. Widen that theme’s candidates
rather than relaxing a rule.
