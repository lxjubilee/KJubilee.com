## ⚠ READ THIS BEFORE YOU RECORD ANYTHING

**The frequency is HM 308.70. It is not 388.70.**

Sixteen files already in the production tree say **388.70**, including all three legal IDs,
five sweepers, the hour clock and `LAUNCH-GATE.txt`. They were written before the
2026-08-25 renumber and never followed it. **Every script in this file says 308.70.** If
you generate from an old file by mistake, you will put a station ID on air that announces
a frequency this station does not broadcast on — which is the one error in radio imaging
that cannot be laughed off.

**And say it out loud before you paste it.** Suno will read `308.70` as "three hundred
eight point seven zero" or worse. Every script below gives you a **SAY** line — the
phonetic spelling that goes in the Lyrics field — alongside the display text. Paste the
SAY line, not the numeral.

> House read: **"H-M three-oh-eight seventy"**
> Never: "three point eight seven", "three-eight-eight", "three hundred eight"

## How to build these in Suno — step by step

Researched against Suno v5 / v5.5 behaviour and against this workspace's own Suno audit
(`BLUEPRINT_AND_SUNO_AUDIT_REPORT_2026-06-03.md`), which is the house authority and is
**stricter than the public guides**. Where they disagree, the house rule wins.

### 0. Before the first generation — make the Voice once

A station's identity is one voice saying the name the same way every time. Suno gives you
two ways to hold that, and in v5.5 the **Voices** button replaced the old *Personas*
button in Create (Style Personas still live inside the Voices menu).

1. Generate **ID-01** first and keep going until one take has the voice you want.
2. On that clip: **⋯ → Create Persona** (or *More Actions → Create → Make Persona*).
   It costs no credits.
3. Name it **`KJubilee — Jubilee (Host)`**.
4. Attach that Voice to every later generation in this file.

**What a Voice does and does not do.** Suno is explicit that a Persona does not lock an
identical singer or a deterministic performance — *you get the same act, not the same
take*. That is fine here and is arguably the point: ten IDs that are recognisably one
station but not ten clones. If you need a literally identical voice, upload a 15-second
to 4-minute reference clip under **Voices** instead.

### 1. Switch to Custom Mode

You need the Lyrics field and the Style field separately. Simple mode will not do this.

### 2. Paste the Lyrics field verbatim

Each script gives you a fenced block. Paste it exactly, brackets and all.

- **Field limit is 5,000 characters** on v4.5+/v5/v5.5. Every script here is under 300 —
  that is deliberate. **Lyric length is the single strongest lever on duration.**
- **`[Spoken Word]`** is the documented tag for spoken-not-sung delivery. `[Announcer]`
  and `[Narrator]` are *not* documented tags — they sometimes work and sometimes get sung.
  Use `[Spoken Word]` and put the announcer character in the Style field.
- **House rule — production cues go INSIDE the bracket**, after an em dash:
  `[Spoken Word — close-mic, warm, unhurried]`. Never leave them as bare parenthesised
  stage directions; the audit scrubbed thousands of those, and Suno reads loose parens as
  lyrics to be sung.
- **House tag whitelist:** `[Intro] [Verse] [Pre-Chorus] [Chorus] [Hook] [Bridge] [Outro] [End]`.
  The audit normalised `[Refrain]`, `[Breakdown]` and `[Chorus 2]` out of the catalogue —
  do not reintroduce them.
- **Never put a timestamp inside a tag.** `[Intro — 0:04 piano]` is a house violation;
  4,176 of those were stripped in one pass. Describe the order, not the clock.
- **End every script with `[End]`.** It is what prevents trailing audio. Where you want a
  tail instead of a hard stop, use `[Outro: fade out]` then `[End]`.

### 3. Paste the Style of Music field verbatim

- **Suno accepts 1,000 characters. This house caps Styles at 800**, with the **first ~200
  characters load-bearing** — Suno weights the front of the field most heavily, so the
  format, the voice and the length constraint go first and the texture goes last. Every
  Style block below is pre-counted and inside the cap.
- **Rule E — no artist or band names. Ever.** The audit scrubbed 764 artist tokens from
  Zariah's files alone (Mahalia ×437), and hundreds more across Caleb, Gabriel and Kingdom
  Pulse. Suno degrades or refuses on named artists, and it is a licensing exposure besides.
  Write the *sound*, not who made it: "arena-worship anthem energy", never "Elevation
  Worship energy".

### 4. Use Exclude Styles for the negatives — not the Style field

Negative instructions inside the Style field ("no drums") are unreliable. The **Exclude
Styles** box under Advanced Options is the actual control. Each script gives you one.

### 5. Getting 8–12 seconds instead of a 3-minute song

This is the whole game with station IDs, and it is won in the Style field. In rough order
of power:

| Phrase | What it does |
|---|---|
| `no verse, no chorus` | **The strongest constraint.** Stops Suno reaching for song structure at all. |
| `single musical phrase` | Keeps it inside one melodic unit instead of developing. |
| `broadcast bumper` | Genre keyword — tells Suno this is programme audio, not music. |
| `instant impact, no intro build-up` | Kills the conventional four-bar run-up. |
| `clean ending hit` | Ends decisively, so the edit point is obvious. |

**Words that will silently make it long — do not use any of them here:** *cinematic, epic,
dramatic, build-up, rising intro, emotional intro, full song, complete track, radio edit,
emotional vocal, melodic vocal.*

**Nobody hits an exact duration in Suno.** Generate three or four, take the closest, and
trim the head and tail in an editor. That is faster than fighting the prompt.

### 6. Land the hook at 0:08–0:12

House standard from the blueprint work. On a ten-second ID that means **the station name
and frequency should be the last thing you hear**, not the first — the ear keeps the tail.
Every script below is built that way.

### 7. Carry the sonic signature

Section 12.8: the three-note motif must appear inside **every** legal ID, sweeper and
persona ID. Each script names which variant it wants —
`signature-full`, `-bright`, `-short`, `-sparse`, `-button` — from `branded/sonic-signature/`.
Until the motif exists as audio, the Style blocks below describe it in words so the
generations are already in the right shape; re-cut them against the real motif once it is
produced.

### 8. Save the file where the tree expects it

Finished audio goes to `branded/legal-id/` as `.mp3` beside its script. A prompt file is
finished when the `.mp3` exists next to it with the same basename — leave the `.txt`
in place, it is the record of what was asked for.

## The ten

Ordered by how much air they should get. **ID-01 through ID-04 are your workhorses**
— they carry name, frequency and slogan cleanly and fit any hour. ID-05 through ID-10 are
dayparted and personality-led; they keep the top of the hour from going stale by the third
listen, which the branded README names as the failure mode that actually kills a station's
imaging.

## Rotation

Section 5.3 wants the brand touching the ear **4–6 times an hour**: the formal ID at the
top, then sweepers every two or three songs. These ten fill the top-of-hour slot only —
see `voice/sweepers/HM308.70.md` for the rest.

| Hours | Rotation |
|---|---|
| 06:00–10:00 | ID-05, ID-02, ID-01, ID-04 |
| 10:00–16:00 | ID-01, ID-03, ID-02, ID-04, ID-08 *(once)* |
| 16:00–20:00 | ID-02, ID-01, ID-04, ID-08 *(once)* |
| 20:00–24:00 | ID-06, ID-10, ID-04, ID-03 |
| 00:00–06:00 | ID-07, ID-06, ID-10 |
| Friday sundown · feasts | ID-09 replaces the first evening slot |

**Do not let one recording own an hour of the day.** The branded README names the real
failure: *"Listeners forgive hearing a song twice in a day. They notice the same eight-word
stinger by the third hour."* Refresh the whole set quarterly (11.3).
